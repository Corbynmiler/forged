## 1. What Forged Is

Forged is a personal commitment app — not another habit tracker.

Here's how it works:
- You set an **Arc**: a 6-week life commitment ("Build the discipline. Ship the product. Stop drifting.")
- You pick **5 proof actions** — the specific daily habits that prove you're becoming who you said you'd be
- Every day you tap each one as you complete it — the ring fills from 0/5 to 5/5
- When the day is complete, Forged writes you a **written receipt** — a daily verdict: "You showed up"
- Over weeks, those days stack into an **evidence spine** — a timeline of proof that this was real

The question Forged answers: *"Did I show up for who I said I'd become?"*

That question is answered in writing, daily. Not with streaks. With evidence.

**Target user:** Someone who has started things and not finished. Who plans but doesn't ship. Who is tired of motivation-based productivity and wants a record instead.

## 2. Marketing Angle

### Primary hook (use this):
> "I nearly skipped today. So I logged proof instead."

### Supporting line:
> "One day doesn't have to be perfect to be on the record."

### Tagline:
> "Proof over promises."

### The idea underneath it:
Motivation is unreliable. Everyone knows that. But proof is recordable — even on a bad day, you can log what you actually did. That record becomes the thing you come back to. Not to feel good. To know it was real.

Forged is not trying to inspire you. It's trying to record you.

## 3. What the First Reel Should Show

The reel tells one story: the proof loop, in real time.

**The arc of the reel:**

| Beat | What it shows | Feel |
|------|--------------|------|
| Hook (0–2s) | 0/5 ring — nothing done yet | Honest, slightly uncomfortable |
| Action (2–6s) | Real app footage: tapping proof items — ring fills | Satisfying, deliberate |
| Complete (6–10s) | 5/5 ring complete, "Today is complete." banner | Earned, clean |
| Receipt (10–12s) | "TODAY'S VERDICT" receipt card — written evidence | Premium, personal |
| End card (12–15s) | Dark background, Forged wordmark, "Proof over promises." | Minimal, confident |

**Tone:** Dark, disciplined, premium. Not motivational. Not flashy. Not TikTok energy. More like a Nike or Apple product spot — but for personal commitment.

**Style:** Real app UI stays the hero at all times. The Higgsfield pass adds premium motion, pacing, and transitions — it does not replace or simulate the UI.
